What's included

More than a session. A development program.

01

Baseline assessment

First session benchmarks technical, physical and decision-making skills so every goal has a starting number.

02

Written development plan

A one-page plan with 2–3 focus areas tied to your player's position and your family's goals.

03

Position-specific sessions

Striker finishing, winger 1v1s, midfield scanning, defender body shape — training the moments they face in games.

04

Session recap for parents

A short text after each session: what we worked on, what clicked, and one thing to practice at home.

05

Home homework

10-minute ball-mastery and wall routines so progress keeps going between sessions.

06

End-of-block retest

We rerun the baseline at the end of the package so you see the before-and-after in numbers and video.
